01 — Meet the studio

A design cocktail mixed
just for you.

We're a small team with a big appetite: strategy, illustration, and brand design served with the right kick. Every project gets mixed by hand — no off-the-shelf recipes.

We work with brands that want to tell a story with character and leave a great aftertaste.

02 — Tasting notes

Our flavor profile

  • 01

    Flavor

    Every brand has its own tone. We distill it until it's unmistakable.

  • 02

    Freshness

    New ideas, not recipes. We push past the obvious.

  • 03

    Color

    Color is a main ingredient, not a garnish.

  • 04

    Structure

03 — Services

The studio menu

  1. A

    Essential identity

    Logo, palette, type, and a tight mini-guide. For brands starting out with good flavor.

    2 — 4 weeks
  2. B

    Full brand

    Strategy, visual system, voice, applications, and a complete brand guide — identity end to end.

    6 — 10 weeks
  3. C

    Rebrand

    For existing brands ready for a new chapter: refreshed art, refined visual system, and new guidelines.

    8 — 12 weeks
  4. D

    Packaging design

    Packaging that looks rich on the shelf and feels right in your hand. Structural and graphic design for full lines.

    4 — 8 weeks
  5. E

    Illustration & art direction

    Editorial illustration, patterns, icons, and art direction with a personality you can taste.

    2 — 6 weeks
  6. F

    Web design & development

    Custom sites and landing pages — UI design, code, and launch — to extend the brand to the web with rhythm and clarity.

    4 — 8 weeks
